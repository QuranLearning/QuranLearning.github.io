Quran Words Picture Lexicon – Production Tool

المعجم المصور لكلمات القرآن الكريم — أداة إنتاج داخلية لسلسلة فيديوهات تعليمية للأطفال تشرح كلمات القرآن الكريم.

This is a private, non-commercial tool used only by its owner, Karim Aly, to produce short educational children's videos that explain words of the Holy Quran.

What the app does

  • Reads source documents, scripts and review sheets that the owner stores in his own Google Drive.
  • Saves generated production files (audio, images, videos, review documents) back to the owner's own Google Drive folders.
  • Uploads finished episodes to the owner's "Published videos" folder in Google Drive.

The app is not offered to the public and has no other users.
