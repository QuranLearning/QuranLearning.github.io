Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy describes how the "Quran Words Picture Lexicon – Production Tool" (the "App") handles Google user data.

Who uses the App

The App is a private tool used only by its owner, Karim Aly. It is not offered to the public.

Google user data the App accesses

With the owner's permission (Google Drive scope), the App reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive: source documents, scripts, review sheets, audio, images and video files of the video series.

How the data is used

The data is used only to produce, review and store the episodes of the series in the owner's Google Drive. The App does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not transfer it to any third party.

Storage and sharing

Files stay in the owner's Google Drive. Access tokens are kept privately by the owner and are used only by the owner's own production scripts. No Google user data is shared with anyone else.

Limited Use

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
